How this works. Fixed cost is plates, makeready time, and the stock burned in makeready. Variable cost is paper, consumables, and run time for the good sheets. Press time is charged at labor plus overhead per hour. Press sheets are rounded up to the next whole sheet. Cost per piece is total cost divided by finished pieces. It's an estimate built entirely on your inputs, it can't know how your shop allocates overhead, so treat it as a starting point for a conversation, not a quote.
